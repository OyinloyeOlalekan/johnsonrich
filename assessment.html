Questions

Is the assessment really free?

Yes. It is a free 30-minute conversation, and there is no obligation to purchase any service.

Is it a full financial review?

No. It is an initial conversation to understand where you stand financially, identify what needs attention first and determine your next step.

Is it for my business or for my personal finances?

For individuals and business owners. Choose whether you are booking as an individual or business owner. If you run a business, we’ll also look at whether your business and personal finances are separated, visible and structured.

Do you give investment advice?

No. JohnsonRich & Co. provides investment education and research, not investment advice or buy/sell recommendations.

Can I ask about your programmes?

Yes. You can also ask about our programmes when you message us. Enrolment in Financial Rewire is by cohort, so ask about the next intake during your assessment.

Do you offer bookkeeping, tax filing, auditing or legal services?

No. Our services do not include bookkeeping, tax filing, auditing or legal services.
